Seeking God

Here's What Stood Out to Me 👇

Seeking God is the most important pursuit in life — not a casual curiosity but a wholehearted desire to know, love, and obey the Creator. True fulfillment isn't found in possessions or achievements, but in a genuine relationship with God.

1️⃣ The Blessed Life — Psalm 119:1–2
"Blessed are those who seek Him with all their heart." Here, blessed means lasting joy that comes from walking with God. Seeking Him involves living by His Word. As Proverbs 28:9 reminds us, ignoring Scripture makes even our prayers detestable. God weighs our motives (Proverbs 16:2), so true seekers align actions and intentions with His truth.

2️⃣ Priorities of the Seeker — Matthew 6:25–34
Jesus teaches that worry cannot add a single hour to life. Instead, He commands: "Seek first His kingdom and His righteousness." When we do, He provides all we need. Worry chokes spiritual growth (Mark 4:18–19), but trust brings peace.

3️⃣ God's Appointed Time and Place — Acts 17:26–28
God determines when and where we live so that we might seek Him. Every divine encounter — a conversation, a meeting, a moment of reflection — is part of His plan to draw us closer.

4️⃣ God Also Seeks Us — John 4:23–24
As we seek God, He also seeks those who worship Him "in spirit and in truth." To worship in spirit means being led by the Holy Spirit (John 6:63); to worship in truth means aligning with God's Word (John 17:17). True worship offers our lives as living sacrifices (Romans 12:1).

5️⃣ Growing Through God's Word — Acts 17:10–12
The Bereans studied Scripture daily to confirm truth. Start with the book of John and use the SOAP method:
🔹 S – Scripture: Write the verse you're studying.
🔹 O – Observation: Note what stands out.
🔹 A – Application: Identify lessons to live by.
🔹 P – Practical: Decide how you'll apply it today.

You can also use SPECK to discover:
🟢 Sins to avoid
🟢 Promises to claim
🟢 Examples to follow
🟢 Commands to obey
🟢 Knowledge to gain

6️⃣ God's Plan for You — Jeremiah 29:11–14
God has a unique plan for every person — one filled with hope and purpose. "You will seek Me and find Me when you seek Me with all your heart." His plans unfold through patient, wholehearted pursuit, not fear or haste.

7️⃣ Guidance on the Journey — Acts 8:26–39
Just as God guided Philip to the Ethiopian eunuch, He orchestrates divine moments for us too. The eunuch's humility, curiosity, and obedience show the joy that comes when one truly finds God.

8️⃣ The Promise to Seekers — Matthew 7:7–8
"Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find." Seeking God is never in vain — those who genuinely seek will surely find Him.

💡 Final Thought
To seek God is to center your entire life on Him — your thoughts, choices, and desires. It's a daily journey of faith, obedience, and transformation. As you study His Word and walk in His ways, you'll find the peace and joy only He can give. Start today — open your Bible, begin with John, and apply the SOAP method. Seek Him wholeheartedly, and you'll discover that He's been seeking you all along. 🙏

How to Know if Your Strategies Are Actually Working

Here's What Stood Out to Me 👇

If you want to know whether your strategies are actually taking effect, here are three principles that make a difference:

✅ 1. Clarify Your Goals and Metrics
Be specific about what success looks like and how you'll measure it. Without clear metrics, even the best strategies can drift off course. Define what progress means before you start so that every effort aligns with measurable outcomes.

✅ 2. Evaluate Your Progress Regularly
Quarterly check-ins (or even monthly, depending on your field) help you stay adaptable and aware. Use these moments to test your assumptions, track progress against benchmarks, and adjust your approach before it's too late. Agility beats intensity every time.

✅ 3. Document Your Processes
What's written can be reviewed, improved, and repeated. Documenting your strategy helps build institutional memory and supports organizational learning. When you record your approach, you create a roadmap that can evolve and scale.

Want to Audit Your Strategies?

Before chasing the next big thing, pause. Many organizations suffer from "Shiny Object Syndrome" ✨ — constantly jumping to new ideas and losing sight of what truly matters.

Before you pivot, ask yourself 🤔:
🔹 Are we failing to meet our initial expectations?
🔹 Has there been a major shift in context or priorities?
🔹 Is there a clearly more promising alternative worth pursuing?

If the answer is no to all three, stay the course and optimize what's already in motion.

The Biggest Lesson
Reflection isn't slowing down — it's how you move forward smarter.
Strategic reflection ensures you're not just working hard, but working effectively toward long-term success.

Discipline, Preparation, and the Meaning of Luck

A personal reflection on success, purpose, and continuous growth

"I am a great believer in luck, and I find the harder I work, the more I have of it." — Thomas Jefferson

The older I get, the more I realize how true those words are.

People often describe success as luck. I see it differently. Luck rarely arrives uninvited—it usually meets those who have spent years preparing for it.

The opportunities that changed my life did not appear out of nowhere. They became meaningful because I had invested time in learning, improving, and staying ready. Hard work doesn't guarantee success, but it dramatically increases the chances of recognizing and acting on opportunities that others overlook.

"We must all suffer one of two things: the pain of discipline or the pain of regret. Discipline weighs ounces while regret weighs tons." — Jim Rohn

Every meaningful goal demands a price. We either pay it through consistent effort today or through regret tomorrow.

Looking back, I can point to moments of failure, rejection, and delay. Some plans never worked. Some goals took much longer than expected. There were times when the results did not reflect the effort invested. Yet every challenge taught me something valuable, strengthened my character, and reinforced the importance of persistence. Progress is rarely a straight line, but it almost always rewards those who refuse to quit.

"We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act but a habit." — Aristotle (commonly attributed)

Excellence is not created by occasional bursts of effort. It is built through the small decisions we make every day—the decision to learn one more thing, solve one more problem, improve one more process, and become just a little better than yesterday.

One of the greatest risks in any journey is complacency. The moment we believe we have accomplished enough is often the moment we stop growing. Success should never become a destination; it should become a platform from which we pursue even greater impact. There is always another skill to master, another challenge to solve, and another opportunity to create value.

For me, work has never been only about earning a living. It is about contributing to something larger than myself. Whether through technology, cybersecurity, research, or innovation, I want my work to leave people safer, organizations stronger, and communities better than I found them. That sense of purpose transforms effort into fulfillment.

I've also come to believe that what many people call luck is often the visible result of invisible preparation. They see the opportunity seized, but not the countless hours spent learning, practicing, failing, adapting, and trying again. The years of discipline are hidden; the results are not.

At the end of the day, we all make a choice. We can embrace the temporary discomfort of discipline or carry the lasting weight of wondering what might have been. I know which path I want to follow.

If success eventually finds me, I hope people call it luck. I'll know it was years of preparation, consistency, purpose, and perseverance that made that "luck" possible.

Discipline creates preparation. Preparation creates opportunity. Opportunity creates what others call luck.

My Readings

Animal Farm: When Revolution Turns Sour

Ever wondered what would happen if animals ran a farm? George Orwell's Animal Farm is a deceptively simple fable that packs a powerful punch. Tired of being exploited by the cruel farmer Mr. Jones, the animals of Manor Farm stage a revolution, driving him out and establishing their own society based on the principles of Animalism: "All animals are equal." Led by the intelligent pigs, Napoleon and Snowball, the animals work together, dreaming of a utopian future free from human tyranny. However, power corrupts. A bitter rivalry develops between Napoleon and Snowball, ultimately leading to Snowball's expulsion and Napoleon's rise as a totalitarian dictator. The pigs gradually seize control, rewriting history, manipulating the other animals, and exploring them even more ruthlessly than Mr. Jones ever did. The seven commandments of Animalism are subtly altered to suit the pigs' selfish agenda. Animal Farm is a chilling allegory of the Russian Revolution, exposing the dangers of unchecked power, propaganda, and the corruption of ideals. It's a reminder that revolutions can be betrayed, and that those who promise equality may become the worst oppressors of all. A timely and unsettling read that will leave you questioning the nature of power and the meaning of freedom.

Oliver Twist: A Child's Struggle in a Cruel World

Imagine a world where poverty is a crime, and orphans are treated worse than stray dogs. That's the London Oliver Twist is born into. This classic novel by Charles Dickens follows the harrowing journey of Oliver, a young boy born in a workhouse, as he navigates the brutal underbelly of Victorian society. Oliver escapes the workhouse and falls in with a gang of child thieves led by the manipulative Fagin. He encounters the Artful Dodger, Nancy, Bill Sikes, Mr Brownlow, and a host of other colorful and often dangerous characters. He yearns for a life of kindness and respect, desperately trying to escape the clutches of the criminal world that threatens to consume him. Dickens masterfully portrays the stark contrast between the grinding poverty and callous indifference of the wealthy and the resilience of the human spirit, even in the most desperate circumstances. Oliver Twist isn't just a story about a boy's survival; it's a powerful indictment of social injustice and a celebration of the enduring hope for goodness in a world rife with cruelty. Will Oliver find the family and security he so desperately craves, or will he be forever trapped by his unfortunate beginnings? Read on to find out!
